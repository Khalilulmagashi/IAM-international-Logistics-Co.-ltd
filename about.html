About IAM

A Nigeria-based trading and supply company

IAM sources and purchases goods, coordinates their import into Nigeria, and supplies them to businesses and projects.

Who we are

IAM International Logistics Co., Ltd. is a private company limited by shares, registered in Nigeria (RC 9490657). The registered office area is Gwale, Kano State.

The company buys goods for customer orders and for selected trading. The four divisions are household goods, construction materials and hardware, machinery and steel.

Ibrahim Auwal is recorded in the company profile as Director and Person with Significant Control.

Vision, as stated in the company profile. To become a leading logistics, procurement and supply chain company in Nigeria and Africa, recognised for reliability, operational excellence and customer-focused delivery.

Mission, as stated in the company profile. To provide efficient procurement, logistics, import/export and industrial supply solutions that support businesses and projects through professionalism and timely execution.
