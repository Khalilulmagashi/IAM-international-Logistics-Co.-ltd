About IAM

Trading, import, industrial supply and logistics

IAM International Logistics is a Nigeria-based trading, import, industrial supply and logistics company. We source and purchase products from China and other markets, coordinate their import into Nigeria, and supply them to businesses, contractors, projects and commercial customers.

Who we are

IAM International Logistics Co., Ltd. is a Private Company Limited by Shares, registered in Nigeria (RC 9490657). IAM is building a commercial trading and supply business that connects international supply sources with Nigerian demand.

Our business combines product trading and supply with procurement, import coordination and logistics capabilities. Core activities are trading, importation, procurement, industrial supply, project supply, logistics, and distribution or delivery. Goods shown on this site are subject to enquiry, specification and availability.

The four trading divisions are household goods, construction materials and hardware, machinery and steel.

Leadership: Ibrahim Auwal is recorded in the company profile as Director and Person with Significant Control (PSC), overseeing strategic operations and service delivery.

Vision (from company profile): to become a leading logistics, procurement and supply chain company in Nigeria and Africa, recognised for reliability, operational excellence and customer-focused delivery.

Mission (from company profile): to provide efficient procurement, logistics, import/export and industrial supply solutions that support businesses and projects through professionalism and timely execution.

What we do not claim

This website does not list unnamed clients, years of experience, Chinese branch offices or government contracts. Where a fact is not in the company profile or registration documents, we use enquiry-based language.
