IAM International Logistics is a Nigeria-based trading, import, industrial supply and logistics company. We source and purchase products from China and other markets, coordinate their import into Nigeria, and supply them to businesses, contractors, projects and commercial customers.

Our business combines product trading and supply with procurement, import coordination and logistics capabilities.

We serve both customer-specific requirements and selected trading opportunities, sourcing products from China and other markets and coordinating their movement into Nigeria.
