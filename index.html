How IAM Trades & Supplies

IAM supplies products against specific customer and project requirements while also identifying selected trading opportunities based on market demand.

01

Customer order / project supply

You have the requirement. We execute the supply.

  1. 01 Customer requirement
  2. 02 IAM purchase / supply
  3. 03 Customer delivery

Requirement → Specification → Sourcing → Negotiation → Purchase → Import → Clearance → Delivery

Customers and projects provide a product requirement, specification and quantity. IAM sources suitable suppliers, negotiates pricing, purchases or procures the goods, coordinates import and clearance processes, and supplies them to the required destination.

02

IAM trading / market supply

We identify the opportunity and supply the market.

  1. 01 Market demand
  2. 02 IAM purchase
  3. 03 Import
  4. 04 Nigerian market
  5. 05 Customers

Market opportunity → Sourcing → Purchase → Import → Clearance → Logistics → Sales

IAM may also identify commercially attractive products, purchase them for its own trading activities, coordinate their import and logistics, and supply them to customers in the Nigerian market.
