How we work

Six steps from enquiry to handover

A visual process you can share with procurement and project teams.

01

Requirement

Customer submits product or project requirement.

02

Sourcing

IAM identifies suitable suppliers.

03

Verification & negotiation

Supplier and product information is reviewed and commercial terms negotiated.

04

Production & inspection

Production and inspection are coordinated where applicable.

05

Shipping & import coordination

Cargo movement, shipping and import documentation are coordinated.

06

Nigeria delivery

Cargo is cleared through the appropriate process and delivered to the agreed destination.
