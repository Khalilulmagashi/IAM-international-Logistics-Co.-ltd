China–Nigeria import coordination

Import & Customs Clearance Coordination

From China to Nigeria, we coordinate the process around your cargo.

IAM International Logistics sources, purchases, imports and supplies goods, and coordinates international shipping, Nigerian port and terminal processes, customs-clearance coordination, transportation and final delivery.

IAM uses its import and logistics capabilities both for goods purchased against customer requirements and for products acquired through our own trading activities. Depending on the transaction, IAM may purchase the goods directly, coordinate procurement on a customer's behalf, or arrange supply from an available source.

Import Documentation Support

Documentation requirements vary by product, transaction and applicable regulations.

  • Commercial Invoice
  • Packing List
  • Bill of Lading / shipping documents

  • Supplier documentation
  • Import-related documentation

  • PAAR-related coordination where applicable
  • SON / conformity-related documentation where applicable
  • Other documents required for the specific cargo
