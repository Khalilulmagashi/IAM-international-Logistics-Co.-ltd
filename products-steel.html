How IAM trades steel

Purchase, import and supply

IAM trades and supplies steel. A requirement can be a customer order, a project specification, a recurring supply arrangement, or a selected product IAM purchases for the Nigerian market.

Source → Purchase → Import → Clearance coordination → Logistics → Supply / sell

Steel can be supplied against specific customer and project orders, as well as through selected trading opportunities where IAM purchases products for market supply.

Large-Volume & Bulk Steel Supply

  • Grade / standard

  • Length
  • Quantity
  • Weight / tonnage
  • Packaging
  • Required delivery location
  • Required delivery schedule

Model 02

Trading & Market Supply

IAM may also purchase selected steel products based on identified market demand and supply them to customers in Nigeria. This is limited to selected trading opportunities, subject to commercial assessment, and subject to availability and market conditions.

IAM does not state that a standing steel inventory is held. Supply is confirmed against the enquiry.
