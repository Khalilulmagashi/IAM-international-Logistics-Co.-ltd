Volume

Large-Volume & Bulk Steel Supply

IAM supports large-volume steel requirements for construction, infrastructure and industrial projects. We can source and purchase steel according to required grade, dimensions, quantity, packaging and delivery requirements.

Large-volume requirements welcome.

Send the following so a quotation can be prepared. Price and delivery time are confirmed only after the requirement is reviewed.

  • Product type
  • Grade / standard
  • Diameter or dimensions
  • Length
  • Quantity
  • Weight / tonnage
  • Packaging
  • Required delivery location
  • Required delivery schedule

Quality & Documentation

Where applicable and requested, IAM can coordinate supplier documentation and product information relevant to the purchase and supply of steel materials. IAM does not manufacture the steel.

Documents are subject to supplier and product documentation. Not every supplier or shipment includes every item below.

  • Product specification
  • Mill Test Certificate (MTC), where applicable
  • Grade
  • Dimensions
  • Weight
  • Heat / batch information, where available
  • Factory documentation, where required
  • Inspection documentation, where applicable

Orders

Project orders and selected trading

Where a contractor or project states the steel, IAM can purchase that material to the stated specification and quantity. IAM may also buy selected steel for its own trading, subject to commercial assessment and market conditions. A standing steel inventory is not claimed.
